How to Bake Bread

Baking a simple loaf of bread at home requires only a few ingredients: flour, water, yeast, and salt. Start by mixing the yeast with warm water and a little sugar, letting it sit until foamy, which confirms the yeast is active.

Combine the flour and salt in a large bowl, then add the yeast mixture and knead the dough for about ten minutes until it becomes smooth and elastic. Kneading develops gluten, the protein network that gives bread its structure and chewy texture.

Let the dough rise in a warm place, covered, until it doubles in size, usually about an hour. Punch it down, shape it into a loaf, let it rise again, and then bake in a preheated oven until the crust is golden brown and the loaf sounds hollow when tapped on the bottom.
